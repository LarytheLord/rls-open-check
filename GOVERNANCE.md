# Governance

rls-open-check is maintained by LarytheLord on GitHub, as part of Guild
(https://www.guilds.work).

- Decisions on rules, output format and releases are made by the maintainer, in
  public issues and pull requests.
- New rules need a test with a failing example and a passing example, and a short
  note on why the pattern leaves data open.
- Changes that could hide a finding (raising a severity threshold, removing a rule)
  are explained in CHANGELOG.md.
- Contributions are welcome under the MIT license in LICENSE.
